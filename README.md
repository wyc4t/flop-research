## Proofline

[Proofline](https://proofline-technocore.vercel.app/) is the separate public evidence explorer and archive developed alongside this research. Its [source, tests, findings and archive documentation](https://github.com/wyc4t/proofline-technocore) remain in their own repository.

Signed records establish bounded claims about keys and payloads. They do not by themselves establish correct work, independent operators, payment or reward eligibility. Devnet payments are test-token observations, not real revenue.
